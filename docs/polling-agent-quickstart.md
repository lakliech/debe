# Polling Agent Quick-Start Guide

**🇰🇪 Linda Mwananchi 2027 — Election Day Field Guide**

> This guide is for polling agents stationed at polling centres on Election Day. Keep it with you.

---

## Before Election Day

### ✅ Your Checklist

- [ ] Your account is created (ask your supervisor for the link)
- [ ] Your role has been set to "Polling Agent" — check with your county coordinator
- [ ] You have completed all required training modules (green ✓ in Training section)
- [ ] Your Code of Conduct has been signed
- [ ] You know your polling station code (ask your supervisor)
- [ ] You have the app installed or bookmarked on your phone
- [ ] Your phone is charged (bring a power bank)
- [ ] You have your national ID and accreditation card

---

## On Election Day — Step by Step

### Step 1: Open the Agent App

Go to: **`[platform-url]/agent/results`**

Sign in with your email and password. You should see your station pre-loaded.

If you can't sign in:
- Check your internet connection
- Contact your supervisor on the emergency line

---

### Step 2: Monitor the Vote Count

- Stay inside the polling station throughout voting (6 AM – 5 PM)
- Note any irregularities in your paper notebook first
- If you see a problem: **do not leave** — note the time, the person involved, and what happened

---

### Step 3: Enter Results (Form 34A)

After counting is complete and Form 34A is available:

1. Open the Agent App (`/agent/results`)
2. Your polling station should appear — if not, search by station code
3. Enter votes for each presidential candidate **exactly as shown on Form 34A**
4. Enter the **total valid votes** and **rejected ballots**
5. The app will check the arithmetic — if it doesn't match, recheck Form 34A
6. Take a **clear photo** of the stamped Form 34A (required)
7. Tap **Submit Results**

> ⚠️ **Double-check all numbers before submitting.** You can submit a correction if needed, but every version is logged.

---

### Step 4: Offline Mode

If there is no internet:
- The app will save your entry locally
- When connectivity is restored, it will automatically submit
- You will see a "Saved Offline" indicator — **do not close the app** until it syncs

---

### Step 5: After Submission

- You will see a **confirmation screen** with your submission reference
- Screenshot it for your records
- Wait for your supervisor's acknowledgement
- Do **not** leave the polling station until the presiding officer releases you

---

## Reporting Incidents

If you witness any of the following, report it immediately:

| Incident | How to Report |
|---|---|
| Violence or threats | Call 999 / your supervisor first, then log in the app |
| Ballot stuffing | Note time + witnesses; report to presiding officer + supervisor |
| Form 34A altered | Refuse to sign; photograph the form; call supervisor immediately |
| Agent excluded | Call supervisor; record the time and name of person who excluded you |
| Equipment failure | Call your supervisor; log in the app |

To log an incident in the app: `/election-incidents` → New Incident
